HISTORICAL BACKGROUND

Maningcol is located 8.2 kilometres from the Municipality of Pinamalayan. The barangay has 527.32 hectares with a total population of 2,047 which is composed of 1,064 males and 983 females. The major economic source is agriculture such as rice farming and other related agricultural products as a source of living.

Maningcol was legally created as an independent barangay, by petition and resolution submitted by the early settlers of the barangay.

The first Barangay Lieutenant of Maningcol was Juan Selda. His councilors were Baldomero Manlises, Esmeraldo Delos Martirez, Luciano Sosa, Marcelo Sotto, Jesus Mahaguay and Venancio Mahaguay.

In 1996, Mr. Selda was succeeded by Marcelo Delmo with councilors Ricardo Sosa, Pepito Palomares, Francisco Malungay and Marciano Malungay.

In 1968, Mr. Delmo was succeeded by Pepito Palomares. His councilors were Antonio Mahaguay, Esming Delos Martinez, Celso Sarmiento and Baldomero Manlises.

It was in 1970 when Lt. Odegario Paderes succeeded Mr. Palomares. His councilors were Ricardo Lianes, Juan De Belen, Antonio Mahaguay and Felecisimo Lolong.

The three-year term of barangay officials began during the term of Antonio Mahaguay in 1972. His councilors were Juan De Belen, Ricardo Mahaguay, Jesus Ondoy, Ben Zoleta, Isidro Mayores and Teosila Soriano.

Under the leadership of Mr. Catapang, three classrooms were constructed and named Maningcol Primary School. Several deep wells were installed. Upon his death he was succeeded by Epifanio Real. To fill the vacancy, Mr. Apolonio Mahaguay was appointed as councilor.

In 1994, Mr. Real was elected as Barangay Chairman and his councilors were Renato Real, Oldarcio de Belen, Winfredo Halamanan, Romeo Delos Martirez Sr., Edgardo De Lara, Pablito Familara and Virgilio Sosa.

On the same election, Juliet S. Pestano was elected as Kabataan Chairwoman and was followed by Geralyn M. Ondoy. Several projects were constructed during his term such as basketball courts, concrete roads and street lights. He finished his three-year term as Punong Barangay in 2007.

In the 2007 election, Hon. Oldarico Manahan De Belen was elected as Punong Barangay. The members of Sangguniang Barangay were Arnold Pestano, Mario Sosa, Felomino Rodas, Victor Mahaguay, Noli Sosa, Ruby Aquino and Danilo Sales. Jaymuel Ondoy was elected as Sangguniang Kabataan Chairperson.

He was succeeded by Victor Mahaguay in 2010. The councilors were Ruby L. Aquino, Mario M. Sosa, Reagan R. Zoleta, Edgardo A. De Lara, Glenda Sosa, Romeo P. Delos Martirez Sr. and Jaymuel D. Ondoy.

In the 2013 election, Hon. Oldarico M. De Belen was elected Punong Barangay for the second time with a new set of councilors. Under his administration, more streetlights were added, a water system was put up in Sitio Centro, roads were concreted, and the school building was rehabilitated with assistance from KALAHI-CIDSS.

Barangay Maningcol was also included in the first batch to receive a Multi-Purpose Building (Covered Court), a project initiative by the Municipal Mayor.

Through the rule of succession, Reagan R. Zoleta took over as Officer-in-Charge for eighteen months.

In May 2020, through the initiative of the people, Judith Pestano De Belen was declared winner with 24 votes more than her opponent Mr. Epifanio Real. She served with the new set of Barangay Officials. Mr. Jhemson M. Pestano was elected Sangguniang Kabataan Chairman.

The Multi-Purpose Building was completed and other projects left by the late Oldarico M. De Belen were completed, including road rehabilitation at Sitio Centro, a farm-to-market road connecting Sitio Bukal Sta. Rita and NIA Road, water systems, solar street lights, road construction and road rehabilitation. Almost 90 percent of barangay roads were completed and rehabilitated.
